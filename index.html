Hey Quark, 🌸

First things first — Happy Birthday, you absolute bolt of a human being. Seriously. I don't think you realise just how rare you are.

I know this past year wasn't smooth sailing. There were things you went through — situations, people, moments — that no one your age should have had to face alone. Things that were heavy. Things that were unfair. And yet — you carried them. Quietly. Strongly. Without ever losing who you are. That alone makes you the boldest girl I have ever seen in my life.

And yes — I know about the storm you quietly survived. You gave everything — your trust, your time, your whole heart — fully and fearlessly. You loved without holding back, and that is not something everyone is brave enough to do. But hear me: what someone else couldn't value says everything about them, and absolutely nothing about your worth. You deserved better. You always did. And the right people — the ones who truly see you — will never make you question that. Keep going, Quark. This is just your beginning. 💫

Also — can we just appreciate for a second how fast you make people feel at home around you? The way you create comfort and closeness in such a short time — that's a gift, not everyone has that. Don't ever let that heart of yours shrink because of someone who didn't know how to handle it properly.

Now I need you to hear this loud and clear — please, please take care of yourself. Eat well. Sleep well. Rest. Be healthy. Be happy. You are the firstborn, and your two sisters are watching you, learning from you, even when you don't realise it. Be their strength, be their safe place — but also remember: you are allowed to have a safe place too. And that's us — your friends. We are here. Really here. Not just on the easy days. Every day. So please, don't be shy to call, to ask, to lean on us. 🤝

And as your dad and mummy always say — pray. 🙏 Let that be your anchor on the hard days. Something bigger is holding you through all of this. Trust that.

So today — let it all go. Every tear, every sleepless night, every moment that hurt more than it should have. That chapter is officially closed. 🔒 Today is a new page. A new year. A new, lighter, happier, healthier Riona.

It's your birthday. Smile. Celebrate. Enjoy every single second of it. 🎉 You've more than earned this day. We love you, Quark. More than you know. 🌸⚛️

With all the love & zero roast (just for today 😄)
